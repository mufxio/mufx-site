Findings council synthesis — editorial, updated per batch

1

Entry discipline is holding. One C-tier entry in the last 32 games (a 26-game clean streak ended by the 低调炫耀 vanguard 8th). Session averages since the entry rules were named: 3.70, 3.88, 4.00 — against 4.4-4.6 before. The tail still lives at champion select, and champion select is now guarded.

2

The audit instrument keeps holding as the sample grows. A-games avg ~2.2 with 100% top-4; C-games avg ~7.4 with zero. Attribution stays inverted: pure-luck excuses in losses remain near zero while ~40% of wins are luck-credited.

3

New behavior: the delayed re-review. The exalted-adventure game was filed as "lost to 5-anima, don't blame myself" — then re-opened 20 minutes later, unprompted, and upgraded into a named alternative line (tank/carry redistribution, reading the opponent's fear). "Its easier to say 5 anima is op than to sit down and think what i actually couldve done. and apparently there is."

4

Contest-fear is converting into contest doctrine. Two days after "i need to stop being scared of contesting," the double-samira game produced deliberate contest warfare: scout the winstreaker, pick the beatable rival, roll before him, take 战力 over half-value contested econ, outlast — 把你熬走. The leak is becoming a weapon; the structural price (no econ afterward) was understood and accepted in advance.

5

The count-cliff is real but state-dependent — Day 7 reversed it. Four straight sessions degraded in the second half; then Day 7 ran 5.43 → 2.57, the best back-half on record ([1,2,3,2] to close), after a long mid-session break and a self-imposed checkpoint at game 7 ('one more, if no good ill rest'). A conditional check-in beat a hard cap: the rule isn't 'stop at 8' — it's 'audit your state at 8 and earn the right to continue.'
