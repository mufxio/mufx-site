About

I spent five years inside full-service hotels and restaurants — front desk and night audit, housekeeping and banquets, long-stay corporate sales at Kempinski Shanghai, and property accounting (AP/AR/GL, budgeting, forecasting, variance) at White Lodging — then underwrote residential mortgages and ran a self-funded land-acquisition campaign. Purdue B.S. in Hospitality & Tourism Management, finance concentration.

Since early 2026 I run muFX, an independent research practice. The method is the same throughout: read the primary document, fix the success criterion before looking at the data, and publish the result with its failures and corrections intact.

Now: targeting analyst roles in hotel real estate — brokerage, advisory, asset management — while continuing the research below. Native English and Mandarin. Open to relocation.

emotion-vector-benchreplication · May 2026 · corrected Aug 17

Reproduced Anthropic's emotion-geometry method on five open-weight models from three labs, on a 24 GB laptop, one command per model.
