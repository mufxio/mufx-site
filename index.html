R2 · retracted 08-06 and 08-08retracted ×2

The acquisition experiment

A +4.75 pp training effect that missed its own pre-registered bar by 0.25 pp — then died twice on audit: a grading defect (corrected estimate −0.58 pp, indistinguishable from zero), and an independent fidelity confound (only 44.6% of test facts existed in both training corpora).

“This is recorded exactly as the rule requires, and is the reason the rule existed: read after the fact, 4.75 pp is trivially arguable into a win.”

IV · The program

What this lab is actually doing

The bet

Most AI work improves capability — math, code, reasoning — against ground truths that can be checked. The underbuilt side is felt-sense: reading what affects people, from the residue they leave in text during real events. The lab's original bet was that this residue is a trainable signal, and that a loop could gather it, train on it, and verify itself against locked bands.

What the record did to the bet

Probing established the substrate is real but marginal, model-specific, and — the closing characterization — not linearly separable from register and event-presence. The fine-tune rung moved nothing (+0.004), so the autonomous loop was cancelled by its own gate rather than run for the demo.

Where it stands

The instrument changed twice: from probing to reconstruction (which beat its baseline only on synthetic worlds), then to corpus-level experiments — does how a training corpus is acquired and arranged change what a model learns? R2 answered nothing, twice, in public. R3 rebuilt the measurement instrument first; its gates are unrun.

What persists

The method. Criteria locked before data, stopping rules obeyed, retractions published where the claim stood, and every number traceable to a results file in one hop. The subject has changed four times. The willingness to say so has not.
