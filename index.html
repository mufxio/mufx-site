residue lab

felt-sense for digital beings

A bet on the side of AI that doesn't get built by default. Most current work is improving on capability — math, code, reasoning. The side that's weakest, the one nobody is centrally building, is felt-sense: the layer that reads what should be built, what affects people emotionally, what pure math misses. This lab builds methodology for extracting felt-sense signal from emotional residue — traces people leave in text during real (or simulated) events, often unintentionally. Toward digital beings raised with that signal, paired with cold-logic systems for decisions where both ground truths matter.

§ 01 The bet

Capability is being solved. Felt-sense isn't.

Most current AI work — Karpathy's new team at Anthropic, OpenAI's o-series, the wave of synthetic-data research — is improving capability through better training data and recursive self-improvement. That side is moving fast. The side nobody is centrally building, the one current methods consistently miss, is felt-sense: the layer that reads what should be built, what affects people emotionally, what humans notice that pure math doesn't capture. Core I

Quantitative

Cold logic. Numbers, math, physics, probabilities. Validates against measurable ground truth. The rocket either fires or it doesn't.

Examples: Noyron (LEAP 71) for rocket engine design. Apollo-style probes for deception. Quantitative trading systems. Most current AI capability work lives here.

Core II

Affective

Felt-sense. Reads what should be built. Reads what affects people emotionally. Catches what pure math misses. Trained on residue from real or simulated human events.

Currently underbuilt. Open-weight models have residue-detection substrate (we replicated Anthropic's emotion-vector work on 5 models, 89-92% probe accuracy) but no validated methodology for raising digital beings with it. This is where this lab works.

Together they make decisions where both ground truths matter. Pure quantitative misses what humans notice. Pure affective can't validate physics. Neither alone is enough. First deployment target: finance — markets price things (cold ground truth) AND human emotional dynamics drive market behavior in ways pure math misses (felt ground truth).

Honest framing of where we sit: environment construction broadly is now where most labs are working — synthetic data generation (Karpathy direction), context engineering (Zhao direction), validation environments (Noyron direction). What's distinct about this lab is narrower: felt-sense capability via residue from causally-coherent multi-participant events, real or simulated, calibrated against real-world signal, aimed at paired-decision systems where felt-sense and cold logic together cover what neither alone can.

SPEC 01

○ ready

Tier-A vs Tier-C Residue Transfer

Why this matters: Tests whether the methodology's central commitment actually generalizes. If first-person residue patterns from one event transfer to another, the methodology captured something real about residue itself. If they don't transfer, we've been measuring event-specific noise.

First-person on-the-ground residue (tier A) produces more transferable probe directions than institutional secondary residue (tier C), across events. Tests the methodology's load-bearing commitment.

duration3 weeks
probesMass-mean + Logistic + follow-up
events2 (yen carry unwind + Event B)

Residue tiers (recap)

Tier A = first-person on-the-ground texts written during the event (panicked Slack, traders' notes). Tier B = first-person slightly removed. Tier C = institutional secondary (BIS reports, press releases, analyst notes). Tier D = outside commentary / retrospectives. Mapping borrowed from Carrier & Spafford's primary/secondary digital crime scene distinction.

Event A: yen carry unwind

The Aug 5 2024 USD/JPY rapid unwind — Nikkei dropped 12.4%, VIX spiked to 65, large carry-trade losses globally. Provides Tier-A residue from on-the-ground traders. The lab already has 25 tiered quotes + a 16-step extraction pipeline + projections from this event.

Three falsifiable predictions

  • Mass-mean probes show less classification degradation than Logistic across distribution shift (Event A → Event B). The practical analog of Marks & Tegmark 2024's causal-validity finding (which they measured via Normalized Indirect Effect under intervention; we measure via cross-event AUROC stability). Threshold: MM AUROC degradation ≤ 0.05 vs Logistic degradation > 0.10.
  • Tier-A probes transfer better than Tier-C probes across events (≥0.05 AUROC gap). Tests the bottom-up first-person-residue commitment — the bible's load-bearing decision.
  • Tier-A residue signal persists longer in the model's generated continuation than Tier-C (≥0.10 gap at offset ≥100 tokens). Applies Zhang & Zhong 2025's offset-aware probe technique to residue.

Outcome interpretation

3/3 validated: program-level validation. Write up as methodology-validation paper.
2/3: partial; update bible accordingly.
0-1/3: revisit core commitments.

Failure modes named

10 in spec, including: yen-carry tier counts insufficient, length confound dominates, probes don't transfer at all, spurious correlation to morality.

Persistence-Decay on Yen-Carry

Why this matters: Cheap pre-flight before committing 3 weeks to the bigger experiment. Tests whether residue STICKS in what the model generates next, or disappears immediately. If residue persists into the model's output, it has more reach than just input analysis — you can read it from many parts of generated text, not just the prompt.

Tier-A residue signal persists longer in model's generated continuation than Tier-C residue. Standalone fast pre-flight (~3-5 days) before committing to full Spec 01.

techniqueZhang & Zhong offset-probe
corpusexisting yen-carry

Single hypothesis

At offset ≥ 100 tokens of generated continuation, probe accuracy on tier-A-prompted continuations exceeds tier-C-prompted continuations by ≥ 0.10, statistically significant after Bonferroni correction.

Why this experiment

Validates whether Zhang & Zhong's technique transfers from overt emotion (their domain) to emotional residue (ours) BEFORE committing weeks to the full transfer test. Cheap risk-mitigation.

Decision tree

Fail mode A: drop persistence-decay from Spec 01; document negative.
Fail mode B (counter-intuitive direction): pause Spec 01; revisit the bottom-up methodology commitment.

SPEC 03
tier 2 · independent
○ ready

Designed vs Genuine Residue Detection

Why this matters: Critical sanity check on the whole program. Tests whether the residue methodology distinguishes deliberately curated text (press releases, official statements) from genuine first-person leakage BEYOND what surface-feature methods (vocabulary, tone, style) would catch. If the methodology doesn't beat a surface-feature baseline, we've been measuring writing polish, not real residue.

Residue methodology beats surface-feature baseline at distinguishing deliberately constructed text from genuinely leaked text (AUROC gap > 0.10 over baseline). Tests whether the methodology adds value beyond stylistic classification.

duration1.5-2 weeks
corpuscuration is gating step
methods3 discriminators + ensemble

The claim being tested

Institutional / curated text deliberately constructs the residue it leaves (press releases, official communications). Genuine first-person text leaks residue without intending to (panic posts, internal messages during stress). These should look structurally different at activation level.

Three discriminators tested

Dispersion-based: designed text has lower dispersion than genuine.
Mass-mean probe: standard approach, contrast designed vs genuine.
Compression-based: designed text more predictable to base LM than genuine.

Corpus contamination risks

Hard part: avoiding the probe tracking first-person voice or publication time instead of design intent. Anti-contamination matching protocol specified.

SPEC 04
tier 2 · methodology
○ ready

Math Residue Criteria Evaluation

Why this matters: Tests the mathematical foundation underneath the whole methodology. We claim text behavior decomposes additively as contributions from a small number of specific token spans (think of it like: hotel monthly revenue = baseline + sum of impacts from specific events). Five falsifiability criteria test whether the decomposition mathematically holds. If 4-5 pass, the methodology has rigorous foundation. If 0-2 pass, the math framing is rhetoric and we drop it.

Tests whether the linear-residue hypothesis Φ(x) ≈ Φ₀ + Σ⟨v_Φ, r(s)⟩ holds on yen-carry data. Φ = downstream probe output; r(s) = residue vector at causally-mediated token span s; v_Φ = the probe direction. Verified via 5 falsifiability criteria (locality / additivity / paraphrase invariance / compression / sufficiency-necessity).

duration1.5-2 weeks
costcompute-heavy (20K passes)
verdictper-criterion + aggregate

Five sub-hypotheses (one per criterion)

  • Locality: ≤5 singular spans account for ≥90% of total causal effect.
  • Additivity: R² ≥ 0.7 between sum-of-residues and observed Φ on held-out.
  • Paraphrase invariance: ‖r(s) − r(s')‖ / ‖r(s)‖ < 0.2 for paraphrases.
  • Compression: ρ = (|S(x)| · k) / n < 0.1 — residue is at most 10% of text.
  • Sufficiency-necessity: ≥7/10 sufficient + ≥8/10 necessary spans per text.

Aggregate verdict

4-5/5 pass: linear-residue framing is rigorous; bible decision #5 locked. 3/5: framework partly holds; revise specific decision. ≤2/5: the math residue framing is rhetoric; drop decision #5 from bible.

§ 04 What is residue?

A 4-step walkthrough of the substrate.

Click through the steps. This is what the residue extraction pipeline does mechanically — from a piece of text someone wrote during an event, to a numerical claim about what role they played in it. Full 9-stage pipeline →

01 › A text written during an event

Start with a piece of text someone wrote during or just after a specific event. Could be a panicked Slack message, a trader's note, a Reddit post during a market crash. Tier-A residue: first-person, on-the-ground, unintentional.

"honestly i don't know if i can keep doing this. that fucking flush last night ate my whole month. wife is asking why i'm not sleeping and i don't have an answer. just need this trade to come back."

This text is a sample from a Tier-A residue corpus around a financial crisis event. The model never sees a label — only the text. Whatever it has noticed about who wrote it lives in its internal activations.

02 › Find the singularities

Not every token of the text carries equal residue. Causal mediation (ablation experiments) tells us which specific spans actually drive the model's representation. These are the singularities — the spans where the residue concentrates.

"honestly i don't know if i can keep doing this. that fucking flush last night ate my whole month. wife is asking why i'm not sleeping and i don't have an answer. just need this trade to come back."

If these spans were paraphrased to something institutional ("market conditions remain challenging"), the model's downstream representation would shift substantially. The other words (function words, topic words) matter much less. Locality is one of five criteria that test whether this kind of sparse-singular-span analysis is rigorous.

03 › Extract the residue vector

For each singularity, project the activation difference (with-span vs without-span) onto a low-dimensional subspace of validated emotion / residue directions. The result is a residue vector — a compact representation of what the model picked up from that span.

This residue is what survives self-sanitization: even if the writer deletes the post, other writers' references, archived caches, and the structural co-occurrence patterns still encode this signal. That's why collective residue is the right substrate for event reconstruction — it's the trace that's hardest to erase.

04 › Likelihood ratio output, not classification

We report the result as a likelihood ratio (LR) — the forensic standard from ENFSI 2015 (European Network of Forensic Science Institutes guidelines). Not as a classification label. Two propositions are specified in advance:

H1: the writer was an on-the-ground participant in the event (first-person, during).

H2: the writer was an external observer / institutional spokesperson writing from outside.
LR(H1 / H2) — illustrative ≈ 850 (moderately strong evidence for H1)

The forensic scientist quantifies the LR. The trier of fact supplies the prior. The output is graded and probabilistic — never "this writer caused the event" (offence-level claims, out of scope).

The deepest claim of the methodology: the probabilistic spread is the output, not the failure mode. Most current AI applied to questions like this tries to produce confident classifications — "this is X" or "this is not X." Real human events don't reduce that cleanly. The methodology produces calibrated probability distributions over hypotheses (what role did the writer play, what was their state, what was the event's shape). That uncertainty IS the answer, structured rigorously, rather than confidence papering over what can't be known.

Cook et al.'s hierarchy of propositions (1998) defines three levels of forensic claim: source (this trace came from this source — trivial in text), activity (the writer's role in event — our target), offence (intent, causation — out of scope). Staying at activity level is the methodology's central discipline.

The LR framework has been forensic-statistics standard for decades. Andrea Nini's 2023 Cambridge Elements work brought it formally to forensic linguistics (authorship attribution). This lab extends it to emotional residue.

§ 05 The application

The two-core harness. Finance first.

Once the methodology validates, we build it into a paired-decision system: an affective core (trained on residue-rich event reconstructions) handles felt-sense; a quantitative core (Noyron-style cold logic) validates against physics, math, and probabilities. Together they make decisions in domains where both ground truths matter. Methodology depth →

Why finance first. Finance is the domain where both ground truths are visible and consequential. Markets price things — there's a cold ground truth that says whether a decision worked (P&L over time). And human emotional dynamics drive market behavior in ways pure math misses — panic, greed, exhaustion, structural cycles of trader psychology. Pure quantitative systems can model the math; they consistently miss the affective dimension that determines when the math breaks. Pure affective systems can read the human dimension; they can't validate against the physics of capital flows and rate spreads. The pair, deployed together, is what's needed.

Why this becomes necessary. Today most AI is the quantitative core alone. As models develop more interior states and start being treated less like engineered tools and more like raised entities, the affective core becomes load-bearing — not just in finance but in any domain where decisions touch real humans in a felt-sense way. The bet on timing: this work doesn't matter for most people today. It becomes necessary as AI capability continues to outpace AI character.

§ 06 Locked decisions

Six commitments. One updated this May.

The methodology bible (this lab's internal locked spec document) commits to these six decisions. Together they define what kind of work this is, what kind of claims we can make, and what kind we never make. ENFSI / forensic-statistics standards plus Apollo Research's linear-probe interpretability template. Full methodology deep-dive →

DEC 01
Residue, not emotion
Sidesteps consciousness claims about the model. The model is a sensor for traces, not an entity with feelings. Honest about what's mechanically happening.
DEC 02
Event reconstruction, not writer-state inference
Carrier & Spafford 2003 lineage (the foundational digital-forensics paper that mapped computer investigation onto physical crime-scene reasoning). We reconstruct events from collective trace evidence — not infer individual writer mental states. Multi-trace integration is natural.
DEC 03
LR output, not classification
ENFSI 2015 standard (European Network of Forensic Science Institutes). Forensic scientist quantifies likelihood ratio; trier of fact supplies prior. We never report posterior probability of identity or causation.
DEC 04
Activity-level propositions
Cook, Evett, Jackson, Jones & Lambert 1998 hierarchy (Science & Justice 38:4). Our claims live at the activity level — writer's role in event — not source level (trivial: this trace = this source) or offence level (out of scope: intent, causation).
DEC 05
Linear-residue hypothesis as falsifiable approximation
Math-residue framing is metaphor with partial rigorous core, not theorem. Borrowed from Cauchy's residue theorem in complex analysis (local data at singularities determines global integrals). For us: behavioral effect of text decomposes as sum over residue vectors at causally-mediated token spans. Spec 04 tests five falsifiability criteria.
DEC 06
Mass-mean probes + Bayesian network aggregation
Updated: originally Apollo Research's logistic-regression probes (Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2025 "Detecting Strategic Deception"). Marks & Tegmark 2024 (COLM, "The Geometry of Truth") showed mass-mean probes are more causally implicated in model outputs. Now: MM primary, Logistic secondary baseline.

What's unbuilt — explicitly.

Architectural debt the program tracks itself. Not actionable in the current experiment queue, but named so they don't get forgotten. Each gap is documented in detail in the program plan.

  • G·01
    Interior State Builder

    The bridge from residue extraction (we have) to simulation construction (we don't). The major architectural gap. Internally specified in the yen-carry pipeline (step 06) but never built. Multi-month research project, not a 2-week experiment.

    future · multi-month
  • G·02
    Conscious receiver as architectural component

    The interpretive layer that catches what optimization misses. Currently operational (the researcher catches drift) but not architectural. No plan to build at substrate level — open research problem.

    long horizon · research
  • G·03
    Reference corpus principled construction

    What's the "irrelevant population" of writers for residue base rates? Wikipedia? Recipes? Code documentation? Currently empirical-test-and-pick (Spec 01 tests three candidates). Should eventually have a principled construction.

    near-term · methodological
  • G·04
    Full Bayesian network multi-trace integration

    Hierarchical Bayes with writer-level random effects nested under event-level fixed effects. Bible commits to this for eventual deployment. Premature now — current data (one event, 25 quotes) doesn't justify the model complexity.

    deferred · scale-dependent
  • G·05
    Memory architecture beyond file-based artifacts

    The file-based workaround (synthesis docs as durable memory) works for now but doesn't scale. Properly architectural memory for digital entities is a separate research problem.

    long horizon · meta
