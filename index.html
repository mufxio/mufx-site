About · third person, chronological

Hao Zhang holds a B.S. in Hospitality & Tourism Management (finance concentration) from Purdue, entered on merit scholarships in engineering. He spent five years inside full-service hotels and restaurants — front desk and night audit, housekeeping and banquets, corporate long-stay sales at Kempinski Shanghai, property accounting at White Lodging — then a year originating and underwriting residential mortgages and a self-funded land-acquisition campaign.

Since 2026 he has run muFX, an independent research practice: a macro data platform with a public API, FX research, and pre-registered machine-learning experiments run on consumer hardware. Success criteria are written down before the data arrives; results are published with their failures and corrections. He works in English and Mandarin and reads Chinese filings in the original.
