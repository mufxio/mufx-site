Site updated2026-08-18

Hao Zhang works at the intersection of markets and machine learning. He holds a degree in Hospitality & Tourism Management with a finance concentration from Purdue University and works in English and Mandarin.

The work is measurement-first. On the markets side: reading primary documents — SEC registration statements, exchange inquiry letters, annual reports, government filings in the original Chinese — against the claims publicly made about them. On the machine-learning side: pre-registered experiments on consumer hardware, with success criteria fixed before any data is seen. Results are published with their criteria, their failures, and their retractions left intact; two headline results on this site were withdrawn by their own author, and the withdrawals are part of the record.
